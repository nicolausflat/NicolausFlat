Frequently asked questions

How far is NicolausFlat from Bari Centrale station?

The apartment is about 400 metres from Bari Centrale station, roughly a 5-minute walk.

How does check-in work?

Check-in is self-service and available at any hour. We send the instructions once your booking is confirmed.

Do you need guests' ID documents?

Yes. Italian law requires hosts to verify guests and register their details within 24 hours of arrival, so we ask for IDs before check-in.

Where can I park near NicolausFlat?

Blue-line street spaces in central Bari are paid (regulated parking zone A). Parking is free from 8:30 pm to 8:30 am and on public holidays. We suggest Interparking Bari Battisti, open 24 hours and about 2 minutes on foot, or Garage Dante at via Dante Alighieri 148.

Is there Wi-Fi and a place to work?

Yes. Wi-Fi is free, measured at 374 Mbps, and there is a desk in the bedroom.

Is there air conditioning?

Yes, there are two units that both heat and cool.

Is the kitchen equipped?

Yes. It has an oven, microwave, induction hob, fridge, dishwasher and Nespresso machine.
