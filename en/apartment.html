Who it suits

Couples, families with one small child, friends and business travellers who want to stay close to the station. The whole apartment is yours: kitchen, living room, bedroom, bathroom and balcony.

Where it is

In the Murat district of central Bari, about 400 metres (5 minutes on foot) from Bari Centrale station and the airport transfers. Via Sparano, the pedestrian shopping street, is nearby. The Petruzzelli Theatre is about 500 metres away (6 minutes on foot). Bari Vecchia, the Basilica of San Nicola, the Swabian Castle and the seafront are a short walk. Supermarkets, pharmacies, banks, a post office and plenty of restaurants are close by.

Check-in and guest registration
